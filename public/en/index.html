About

I’m Ahror Alikhanov, a senior graphic and brand designer based in Tashkent. My main focus is branding, visual identity and art direction. I build complete visual systems — from logos to packaging, print and digital materials.

I have experience across retail, healthcare, education, construction, restaurants and services. On every project I focus on understanding the business’s goals, audience and what makes it unique. In design, I value a clear idea, a systematic approach, functionality and attention to detail.
